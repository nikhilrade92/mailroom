Mailroom

A minimalist desktop mail client for Gmail

Mailroom is a personal desktop app for Windows that lets you read, write and organise your Gmail in a calm three-pane window.

What it does

  • Read, search, compose, reply to and forward mail, with attachments and drafts.
  • Organise mail into nested folders (Gmail labels), by drag and drop.
  • Create rules that move, flag, mark or delete incoming mail automatically.
  • Use several Gmail accounts, light and dark themes, and your own background.

How it uses your Google account

Mailroom runs entirely on your own computer and connects directly to Gmail. It asks for permission to read, send and organise your mail, and to manage your Gmail filters, because those are the features of the app. It has no servers of its own, and your mail is never sent anywhere other than between your computer and Google.
