Mailroom

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Mailroom is a desktop mail client that runs on your own computer. This page explains what it does with your information.

What Mailroom accesses

When you sign in with Google, Mailroom asks for two permissions:

  • Read, compose, send and organise your Gmail (gmail.modify), to show your mail, send messages you write, and move, label, flag or delete messages when you ask it to.
  • Manage basic mail settings (gmail.settings.basic), to create, show and remove the Gmail filters behind the rules you set up.

How your data is used

Your Gmail data is used only to provide the features you see in the app. Mailroom has no servers. Your messages travel only between your computer and Google, and are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Mailroom does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, does not use it to train AI models, and does not allow any person to read it.

What is stored, and where

  • Your Google sign-in tokens and OAuth client details are stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage.
  • Your preferences (theme, background, pane sizes, rule names) are stored on your computer.
  • Messages are loaded from Gmail when you view them and are not kept in a separate database by Mailroom. Attachments are saved only where you choose to save them.

Removing access and deleting data

Signing out of an account in Mailroom revokes its access and deletes its tokens from your computer. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Uninstalling Mailroom and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mailroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions can be sent through GitHub.
